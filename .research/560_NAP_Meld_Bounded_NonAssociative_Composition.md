## TL;DR

Murphy proposes the **Neural Admissibility Program (NAP)**: algebraic properties of language — **non-associative hierarchical grouping, commutativity, recursive closure, access to substructures, structured workspace transitions** — specify *invariants* that any mechanism implementing structure-building must preserve, and predictive (decoding) success alone cannot adjudicate between candidate mechanisms. He corrects the Marcolli–Berwick entropy-optimized binding gate (arXiv:2305.18278 / 2507.13501 — the Rényi-entropy semiring), showing it preserves grouping only in a narrow commitment band, and proposes **Meld**: two constituent populations **converge through shared synapses, integrate sublinearly, and saturate** — claimed the closest neurally plausible composition law to syntactic Merge, preserving every NAP invariant, recovering hierarchy at every tested depth/temperature, with two testable predictions: **effective population dimensionality separates alternative bracketings**, and **the composite depends on constituent disagreement**.

**Distilled for katgpt-rs (modelless, inference-time):** the payload is algebra + closed-form dynamics + an eval methodology — no training anywhere (no optimizer/loss/RL; simulations are ODE-style population dynamics ⇒ adversarial panel not triggered). The actual law (paper §4.9.1, verified): **`Meld(u,v) = tanh(κ·W·[λ⋆u + (1−λ⋆)v])`**, κ=2, where **λ⋆ is the offset-corrected Rényi gate's argmin** — a *pointwise soft minimum* (`λ⋆ = argmin_λ [λu + (1−λ)v + (1/β)·log(λ²+(1−λ)²)]`, S₂(λ) = −log[λ²+(1−λ)²]) that rewards daughter agreement and penalizes disagreement — passed through **tied orthogonal weights W** (the "shared synapses"; tying IS the commutativity requirement — untied weights leak daughter order) and a **saturating transfer**. The paper's own ablation hierarchy is the load-bearing finding for us: the soft-min (disagreement-sensitivity) and the tied non-permutation W (lifts bracketing separation from 4th to 2nd order) are *forced by the algebra*; **the saturation contributes ONLY boundedness** ("any bounded nonlinearity would be appropriate"; divisive normalization performs equivalently/slightly better at depth; the *unsaturated* linear tied mixture "law 8" matches Meld on every axis: 1.000/1.000/0.998/0.964/0.899 across d=1–5).

Two extractions: (1) **NAP-admissibility audit** — a 5-invariant battery for composition mechanisms, which when pointed at OUR stack proves a real gap: *every shipped composer is bracketing-blind* (mass-weighted mean, EMA lerp, gated sums, linear blends are associative/commutative ⇒ grouping and order information is provably destroyed; TPR preserves structure only via explicit role bookkeeping, not via its law). (2) **`meld`** — a bounded, commutative, non-associative, disagreement-coding composition primitive (the law above). Bounded magnitude under arbitrary composition depth (fixed-size Pod f32 safety), grouping retention, disagreement trace (λ⋆ → min-like as β·|u−v| grows: the composite stops averaging where constituents contradict).

## 1. Paper Core Findings (PDF-verified 2026-09-15)

1. **The Neural Admissibility Criterion (NAC).** A theory F with objects S, operations M_k, invariants I_j; an implementation is an encoding N: S → X plus physiological ops G_k, admissible only if the relevant diagrams commute up to stated equivalence and the **invariants I_j remain recoverable under N**. "Mere correlation with a variable defined by F is not sufficient to establish implementation" — the methodology mirror of our §3.6 (architectural ≠ quality parity) and withheld-pair OOD discipline.
2. **The three-fold failure of linear mixtures** (§4.9): (a) nothing fixes the mixture weights Λ (a family of maps, not a map); (b) with Λ free the output leaves the type (closure fails at the first step); (c) the λ-side vs (1−λ)-side is a *planar* structure — it orders the daughters (non-commutativity smuggled in). Fixing λ=½ (mean-pooling) discharges all three but is **content-independent** — "part company only on contrasts that a fixed mixture cannot resolve."
3. **The extensivity correspondence** (thermodynamic-semiring axiom correspondence, after Marcolli–Thorngren 2014): ⊕_{S,β} commutative ⟺ S symmetric; associative ⟺ **S extensive**; all three axioms together ⟺ S is Shannon. **Non-associativity IS the failure of extensivity** — the entropy of a grouped mixture not recoverable from its parts. "A candidate neural gate that is symmetric in its daughters and non-extensive in its scoring is thereby a candidate Merge; one that is extensive is not, whatever else it does." Mean/sum/EMA composers are Shannon-extensive ⇒ **provably inadmissible** as structure-preserving composers.
4. **Rényi order selection**: α=2 is the largest order at which the gate's variational problem is convex in λ; above α=2 the minimizer is *discontinuous* (jumps of 0.05–0.39 as daughter difference crosses a threshold) — disqualifying for bounded noisy dynamics.
5. **The pointwise-class theorem**: every pointwise, commutative, translation-equivariant gate has the form `g(x,y) = (x+y)/2 + h(|x−y|)`; a balanced depth-d tree evaluates to `A + d·h(0)`, bounded in depth exactly when idempotent; the balanced four-leaf pair is separated only at **fourth order** (ε⁴) by this whole class. Mean-pooling: leaf mixture `a_ℓ = 2^−depth(ℓ)` fixed by tree shape alone ⇒ **any two bracketings with the same leaf-depth multiset are provably indistinguishable** (identical S₂, identical D_eff).
6. **Meld** (§4.9.1): `tanh(κW[λ⋆u + (1−λ⋆)v])`, κ=2 — soft-min mixture λ⋆ (rewards agreement, penalizes disagreement) + tied orthogonal W (commutativity; lifts four-leaf separation to **second** order via the `(A−D)∘(B−C)` cross term, vanishing only for permutation W) + saturating transfer (**boundedness only** — any bounded nonlinearity; divisive normalization equivalent/slightly better at depth; unsaturated "law 8" `W[λ⋆u+(1−λ⋆)v]` matches on every axis). Depth ladder: bracketing 1.000/1.000/0.998/0.970/0.922 (d=1–5); law 8: 1.000/1.000/0.998/0.964/0.899; removing W: 0.843/0.754/0.705/0.677/0.684 (W is load-bearing). Inadmissible ordered role-filler law: 0.972 at d=5; recurrent law (untied weights): 0.799. **No temperature window**: ≥0.99 for β ∈ [0.03, 30] (β→0 degrades to tied-weight unit, β→∞ to saturated tied minimum — both carry structure).
7. **Effective dimensionality readout** (§9.2): the collision entropy IS the log participation ratio — `S₂(A) = −log Σ_ℓ a_ℓ² = log D_eff(A)`. Honest caveat inside the paper: the *deterministic* superposition Ψ(T)=Σ a_ℓΨ(ℓ) has power a_ℓ², so PR over the squared-amplitude distribution gives `exp(3S₄ − 2S₂)`, NOT exp(S₂) — the clean identification needs variance linear in mixture weight (stochastic spiking in cortex; a design constraint for any of our deterministic readouts).
8. **Division of labor** (§6.7): expander-Hopfield store = the memory/workspace; threshold-linear (CTLN) sequencer = the derivation scheduling; the composition gate = the binding step. Structural mapping to our substrate: ShardIndex (BLAKE3-keyed content-addressable zone→shard) ≈ the workspace store; phase_gate/mape_k ≈ the sequencer; `meld` ≈ the binding gate. Plus §5's phase-address code + order-free hash-key — a content-addressable memory via hash keys is literally ShardIndex's access pattern.

**§4 searches (subagent, 11 searches, 2026-09-15):**
- "Meld" binding law: no prior art under that name (closest: Swan & Wyble binding pool 2014 — resource sharing, no composition law; bioRxiv 2026.02.18.706604 — compares six binding ops, proposes none; ROSE arXiv:2303.08877 — different machinery). **The paper's own precursor list (§4.9.1, read in PDF) confirms the conjunction-only novelty claim**: VTB (Gosmann & Eliasmith 2019) is non-associative but **non-commutative**; Assembly Calculus Merge (Papadimitriou et al. 2020) creates assemblies without the invariants; sublinear dendritic integration for feature binding is experimentally established (Cazé et al. 2024; Tang et al. 2025); divisive normalization is a standard bounded-integration motif (Carandini & Heeger 2012; Ohshiro et al. 2011); Reimann 2025 shows non-associativity is computationally useful for temporal structure. "Meld... does not introduce a novel physiological primitive; its novelty lies in showing that familiar neural primitives, when jointly constrained by the algebra of the computation they must realize, converge on a previously unarticulated binding law" — bounded + same-space + content-dependent + non-associative + exchange-symmetric + disagreement-sensitive-at-fixed-summed-drive, "no previous neurally interpretable binding law has been derived to satisfy this full conjunction."
- VSA binding ops (Kleyko et al. survey arXiv:2001.11797; VTB Gosmann & Eliasmith 2019): all bilinear/elementwise, invertibility-oriented; **saturation is normalized away, not embraced** — Meld not anticipated.

### Closing verdict

**The census finding survives code-level scrutiny, sharpened by the theorems.** No shipped composer combines saturation + sublinearity + substructure access + disagreement-coding — each axis is individually absent from every law: (i) *saturation* exists only as parameter preprocessing (the blend's σ gates on committed logits) or as convex-combination boundedness (the lerps); tpr and the gauge sum are magnitude-unbounded; (ii) *sublinearity in the constituents* is absent everywhere — every shipped law is a linear functional of its constituents (flat sum, weighted sum, mean, lerp); (iii) *substructure access* exists only through bookkeeping external to the law (tpr's role slots + unbind, exact or crosstalk-bounded) or trivially (the backup sketch is stored, the sparse mask is readable) — never produced by a composition dynamics; (iv) *disagreement-coding into a composite* is absent — the stack's four disagreement signals (gauge cancellation prune, DSOM heal error-scale, wedge span, jury spread) either delete, scale a step size, rank candidates, or refuse — none leaves a trace inside a merged state. And the common composers are *provably* bracketing-blind, not merely unaudited: Raven's sleep-mean and the α=0.3 EMA are exactly the paper's fixed-λ mean-pooling class (identical composites on same-leaf-depth-multiset bracketings, §1.5); the blend's frozen gates are per-archetype-independent — content-independent across constituents — so the same theorem lands it in that class; the gauge sum is a free-Λ linear mixture with planar daughter-typed weights (folds a+c of §1.2); tpr's sum is associative-by-law and saves structure only through the F3 bookkeeping route. The gap Meld targets is therefore real at the code level: a bounded, commutative, non-associative, disagreement-coding law with law-native substructure access has no shipped counterpart — and the two nearest neighbors (gauge compose, DSOM-scaled heal) each hold exactly one Meld ingredient (a nonlinear prune / a disagreement-scaled gate) while lacking the other three.

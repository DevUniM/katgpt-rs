# INTENDED convention. numbering_floors.txt says so in the file, measured
# 2026-09-04, and reporting them would have been ~52 false findings out of the
# gate that exists to stop exactly that.

# ⛔ 61 of the 70 are the PRE-GATE archive — the number-recycling `.issues/121` documents, from
# before the monotonic rule existed — and adjudicating them is a backlog, not a
# pin. Issue 785's rule forbids ratcheting a bucket that means "unread", so
# they are RATCHETED as a count and never pinned with invented reasons.
#
# Every one of the 9 at or above the boundary is from ONE event, and above the
# boundary the wall is absolute: a new collision there is a live ambiguity in
# prose people are writing today.
#
# ── the boundary is MEASURED, not chosen for roundness ─────────────────────
# 700 is where the two populations separate cleanly: the highest legacy
# collision is `.issues 575` and the lowest divergence one is

# it by a judgement call. Re-measure before moving it.

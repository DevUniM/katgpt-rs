## Why this issue exists

The cross-arch canonical intent claim (Proposal 009) was permanently demoted after four hidden-state methods failed (Bench 422/424/425/426/427 — best cross-arch agreement +0.009 at k=16 against a ≥ 0.5 threshold). Research 459's closure names exactly ONE reopen path: a construction external to all models. Proposal 010 sketches it — deterministic Rust source features (AST node-type histogram first; clippy fingerprint + ownership graph only if the first family shows signal) projected into each model's latent space via a per-model ridge-fit adapter, reusing the shipped `katgpt-canon` substrate (Bench 562 GOAT, intra-arch).

The proposal self-assesses HIGHLY SPECULATIVE — it exists because it is the only remaining path, not because success is likely. This issue scopes the cheapest falsification sequence.

## ⛔ Corpus finding (verdict round 1) — the Recipe D corpus is NOT a usable fixture

`riir-train/data/canon_recipe_d/pairs.jsonl` is **Rust↔Python** 7-token fragments (`{"rust_prompt":"let c = &cell;","python_prompt":"r = x",...,"idiom_tag":"ownership"}`), NOT idiomatic-vs-non-idiomatic Rust. Reusing it would be worse than having no fixture: `syn` parses Rust and not Python, so an AST histogram separates the two arms perfectly and trivially — both models separate Rust from Python, the extractor separates Rust from Python, and the independently-fit projections agree for a reason with nothing to do with steering. That is proposal caveat 2 (the trivial-signal false positive) in its purest form: a green G5 on a null claim. `syn::parse_file` also wants items and fails on both arms of a 7-token fragment; a histogram over one has one or two non-zero bins, while proposal G2 gates the extractor at 10K-line-crate scale. **T1 therefore requires a NEW Rust-only contrastive corpus** (riir-train Issue 567), generated by the same `canon_p4_generate_corpus` machinery with new candidate pools seeded from the 10 `idiom_tag` families. Comparability against Bench 427 comes from reusing the **protocol** (k ∈ {2,4,8,16} sweep, train/test split discipline, the detrend sanity check, the paired-forward-pass harness), never the corpus.

## Honest caveats (carried from the proposal — READ FIRST)

1. **Coarse-features risk is the top risk** — AST histograms may carry too little semantic signal; T1 is priced in hours precisely so a dead end costs hours.
2. **False-positive mode:** both models may "agree" trivially because both prefer well-structured code. T3's contrasted-direction requirement is the defense — do not weaken it to get a passable number.
3. **`syn` dep weight** stays opt-in-gated (the BOUNDARY.md row in T1); the canon crate's default surface must not grow.
4. Promotion, production steering runtime, and cross-language directions are all OUT of scope — this issue validates or kills the construction, nothing more.
